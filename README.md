# Positive Candle Co. — Candle Calculator

A simple candle-making calculator for working out wax and fragrance quantities. Works in your browser on a computer, tablet or phone.

## Calculation options

- **Start with candle quantity:** Calculate the wax and fragrance needed for a chosen number of candles, including optional wastage.
- **Start with wax quantity:** Calculate fragrance required, the number of whole candles and excess mixture.
- **Start with fragrance quantity:** Calculate wax required, the number of whole candles and excess mixture.

Sections 2 and 3 also show excess mixture as a wastage percentage of the total mixture.

## Fragrance blending

Choose a single fragrance or blend two or three fragrances together.

For blends, enter fragrance names and your preferred percentages. Percentages must total **100%**. The calculator displays each fragrance’s weight in whole grams, balanced to add up to the rounded fragrance total.

## How to use

1. Choose the section that matches your starting quantity.
2. Edit the pale yellow fields.
3. Read the automatically updated results in the grey fields.
4. Select a fragrance blend option if required.

Use **Reset defaults** to restore the starting values or **Print calculator** to print your results.

## Calculation notes

- Fragrance percentage is based on the **total finished mixture weight**, including wax and fragrance.
- Calculated gram quantities display to the nearest whole gram.
- Candle counts round down to whole candles.
- Wastage percentages in sections 2 and 3 equal excess mixture divided by total mixture, multiplied by 100.

## Opening the calculator

Visit the website published through GitHub Pages, or download `index.html` and open it directly in your browser.

No installation or account is needed to use the calculator.
